our story

The name says it all

"Ghorer Ranna" means home cooking in Bangla and that's exactly what this is. Prepared with love, in small batches, with top-quality ingredients. An aroma and taste magical enough to transport you to Bangladesh.

One day, a single father stepped into the kitchen for the first time to replicate the dishes he grew up loving. With a bit of perseverance and a lot of heart, he cracked the code to his familiy's traditional recipes, and became his childrens' favourite cook. Seeing their friends struggle with school, work, and meal prep, he decided to share his magic with them, and thus Ghorer Ranna was born.
